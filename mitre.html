ℹ️ About MITRE ATT&CK Mapping

SentinelAI maps detected behaviors to MITRE ATT&CK techniques to provide security analysts with standardized information about attacker behavior. Current detection coverage includes Network Service Scanning (T1046), Brute Force (T1110), and DNS (T1071.004), and Direct Network Flood (T1498.001).
